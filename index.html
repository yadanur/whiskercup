Whisker Cup Support

Whisker Cup: Cat Soccer is a 4-a-side soccer game with cats for iPhone.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email y.adanur@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

There's no sound. The game follows the iPhone's silent switch. Turn silent mode off and turn the volume up.

The buttons are in an awkward spot. Open the menu or pause the game, tap Move buttons and drag KICK and PASS where you like.

Does it need the internet? No. The game works fully offline and has no ads or accounts.

Privacy

Whisker Cup collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
