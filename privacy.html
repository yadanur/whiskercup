Whisker Cup Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Whisker Cup is a cat soccer game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

We don't collect any data

Whisker Cup does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

What stays on your device

The game saves two small things on your own device only: your win and loss record, and where you placed the KICK and PASS buttons. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
